# 1. Executive Summary

## The Opportunity

Saudi Arabia's PIF-owned real estate giga-projects — Diriyah, ROSHN, Qiddiya, and NEOM — represent one of the largest concentrations of off-plan real estate development in history. These projects demand world-class digital sales tools, immersive visitor experiences, and intelligent property platforms that can operate at scale across international markets.

**Publicis Sapient** brings enterprise-grade AI (Bodhi platform), agentic workflow orchestration, data architecture, and an existing relationship with Diriyah Gate Development Authority (DGDA) and NEOM.

**VMI Studio (Journey)** brings photorealistic real-time 3D, Unreal Engine digital twins, 3D Gaussian Splatting, immersive room installations, and deep experience delivering experiential sales tools for Saudi giga-projects including the Trojena digital twin for NEOM.

**Together**, the joint venture can deliver something neither can alone: **AI-powered, photorealistic digital twin sales platforms** — where an intelligent agent (Bodhi) guides buyers through a cinematic-quality 3D property experience (VMI), personalises the journey in real-time, and converts interest into transactions.
