## 3.1 Company Overview

Publicis Sapient is the digital business transformation arm of Publicis Groupe, operating with **SPEED capabilities**: Strategy, Product, Experience, Engineering, Data & AI. They focus on enterprise-scale digital transformation across industries.

## 3.2 Bodhi — Enterprise Agentic AI Platform

Bodhi is Sapient's flagship AI platform, purpose-built for deploying autonomous AI agents in production at enterprise scale.

### Architecture (Three-Tiered)
1. **Infrastructure Layer** — Data ingestion (ERP, CRM, APIs), data shaping, AI model hosting (proprietary + open-source LLMs), security/compliance (GDPR, HIPAA, SOC 2)
2. **Pre-Built Capabilities** — Agent orchestration, governance, shared context/memory across agents, business rules enforcement
3. **Solution Building** — Custom agentic workflows integrating with SAP, ServiceNow, Salesforce, JIRA, Confluence

### Key Differentiators
- **Framework-agnostic** — no cloud or model lock-in
- **Governance-first** — agents operate within business rules, regulatory constraints, and audit trails
- **Shared memory** — agents maintain context across interactions and handoffs
- **Production-proven** — 75% faster delivery cycles, 50%+ cost reductions, 99% code-to-spec accuracy

## 3.3 Immersive / Digital Twin Track Record

### Renault Digital Twin (Flagship Case)
Publicis Sapient partnered with Renault to build a **full digital twin of their Champs-Elysees showroom** using Unreal Engine Pixel Streaming:
- Photorealistic, interactive 3D environment accessible via web browser
- Personalised avatars, real-time chat, integrated microphone
- 125 years of brand history in immersive 3D
- Six languages (French, English, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, German)
- Modular architecture for future commercial extensions

### ViCE-X Virtual Conferencing
Built a virtual conferencing platform combining data, AI, and personalisation for immersive digital conference experiences.

### Spinnaker SCA (Supply Chain Digital Twins)
Acquired Spinnaker SCA bringing digital twin capabilities to supply chain optimisation — 15% forecast accuracy improvement, 77% reduction in parts shortages.

### Gap: No Real Estate Digital Twin Work
**Critical finding**: Publicis Sapient has demonstrated strong Unreal Engine and immersive capabilities but has **no public track record of digital twin work in real estate sales**. Their digital twin experience is in automotive (Renault) and supply chain (Spinnaker). This is precisely the gap VMI fills.

## 3.5 What Sapient Wants to Explore

Based on their public positioning, partnerships, and capability investments, Sapient is clearly pursuing:

1. **Agentic AI at Scale** — Bodhi is their bet. They need compelling vertical use cases beyond content and supply chain.
2. **Immersive Digital Experiences** — The Renault twin proves they value this, but they lack an in-house 3D/real-time team at VMI's calibre.
3. **Saudi Arabia Growth** — They have DGDA and NEOM but want to expand across the PIF portfolio. Real estate sales is a massive adjacent market.
4. **"Cognitive Places"** — Their own language for digital twins that think. They want to bring AI into physical/digital environments.
5. **Measurable Business Outcomes** — Every Sapient case study leads with metrics (conversion, cost reduction, speed). They want partners who can tie immersive experiences to revenue.
