# 4. VMI Studio (Journey) — Capabilities & Saudi Presence

## 4.1 Company Overview

**VMI Studio** was founded in London in 2012 by **Fergus Byrne** and **Nicholas Byrne** — a collective of architects, 3D designers, and reconstructive archaeologists. Grew to a 60-person studio recognised as one of the UK's fastest-growing creative technology companies.

### Acquisition by Journey (December 2024)
VMI was acquired by **Journey** (journey.world) alongside **59** (a story-driven design studio). Journey is a global design and innovation studio pioneering **Multidimensional Experiences (MDX)**.

- **Combined entity**: 250-strong global team
- **CEO**: Andrew Zimmerman
- **Disciplines**: Strategy, design, architecture, video, technology, narrative
- **Claim**: First truly multidimensional experience studio spanning entertainment, healthcare, retail, hospitality, financial services, real estate, and cultural institutions

### Business Mix
~70% high-end property developers, ~30% arts and entertainment

## 4.2 Technology Stack

### Unreal Engine (Core)
VMI uses Unreal Engine as its primary platform for virtual twins and real-time 3D, producing photorealistic renders and interactive experiences. Proven on city-scale projects (Trojena, Here East).

### Luna Interactive Platform (Proprietary)
VMI's marketing platform for real estate developers:
- **Interactive maps** — 2D, illustrated, 3D city-wide models
- **Orbit flights** — aerial fly-through experiences
- **Detailed analytics** — customer journey tracking, view rates, forward sharing
- **Live CRM feeds** — Salesforce integration for apartment availability and pricing
- **Data dashboard** — real-time sales intelligence

### 3D Gaussian Splatting
VMI uses 3DGS as part of its capture and delivery pipeline — enabling photorealistic scene capture from photos/video for web delivery.

### Immersion Room Technologies
Purpose-built R&D lab at VMI HQ in London developing custom immersive room installations for real estate sales centres. These are physical spaces with projection, interactive surfaces, and VR integration.

### Virtual Reality
Custom VR experiences where buyers walk through unbuilt properties using headsets and controllers. Proven on Saudi giga-project fly-throughs.

### CGI Visualisation
Photorealistic exteriors, interiors, architectural visualisations, photo-montages, verified views, animations, and multimedia apps.

3. **Luna platform** — existing analytics + CRM integration layer for real estate sales
