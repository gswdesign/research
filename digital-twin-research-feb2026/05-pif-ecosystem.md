# 5. PIF Real Estate Ecosystem — Diriyah, ROSHN, Qiddiya

## 5.1 Public Investment Fund (PIF) Overview

PIF is Saudi Arabia's sovereign wealth fund — now exceeding **US$1 trillion in assets** (late 2025), ranking as the 4th-largest SWF globally. It has launched **100+ companies** across diverse sectors, with its workforce growing from ~40 employees (2015) to ~4,000. PIF is the engine behind Vision 2030's diversification strategy, and its real estate portfolio includes some of the most ambitious development programmes in history.

### PIF Digital Transformation Context
- PIF giga-projects are entering **active sales and pre-leasing phases** in 2025-2026
- International buyer audiences require **remote, multilingual** sales tools
- Saudi Arabia is emerging as a **global leader in PropTech** adoption, alongside Dubai
- The Kingdom's emphasis on tourism (targeting 150M visits by 2030) demands **immersive digital previews** of destinations not yet built
- At **LEAP 2025**, PIF-backed deals worth **US$14.9 billion** were unveiled (Groq, Lenovo, Databricks, Salesforce)
- **HUMAIN** launched in 2025 to turbocharge AI innovation across healthcare, manufacturing, energy
- PIF + SITE + **Microsoft** signed an MoU for sovereign cloud services in Saudi Arabia (Nov 2025)

### PIF Real Estate Digital Infrastructure
- **RER** (National Real Estate Registration Services Company) — wholly PIF-owned, digitising all property records. Target: 80% of Kingdom properties registered by 2025. Advancing geospatial mapping for precise boundary identification.
- **FMTECH** — PIF-launched national facilities management company (2023), partnered with **JLL** (2025) for advanced digital facility management platforms
- Saudi real estate market projected to reach **$101.62 billion by 2029** (8% CAGR)
- At Cityscape Global 2025, real estate agreements exceeded **SR 161.2 billion ($42.3B)**

## 5.2 Diriyah Gate Development Authority (DGDA)

### The Project
Diriyah is positioned as Saudi Arabia's cultural capital — a 14km² heritage-driven mixed-use destination at the birthplace of the Saudi state. It includes luxury hospitality, residential, retail, cultural institutions, and the UNESCO World Heritage site of At-Turaif.

### Digital Presence (Current)
- **Publicis Sapient** built 4 websites in 18 weeks (record-breaking Sitecore implementation)
- 1M+ visits in first 6 months
- 200+ components, 252 content types, 50+ templates

### Existing Digital Twin & Immersive Work
- **IMERZA** (Epic Games' official US partner studio for Digital Twins) has created large-scale architectural digital twins for Diriyah Gate using Unreal Engine. IMERZA was acquired by Together Group in Feb 2025 and integrated into Noe & Associates Studios (offices in London, New York, Dubai).
- **Diriyah Immersive at Outernet London** — DGDA partnered with Chrome Productions and Immersive-me to create a 5-room multi-sensory installation at Outernet London (world's largest wraparound video screen). ~20 minutes of bespoke content transporting visitors through Diriyah's past, present, and future.
- **Diriyah Gate Experience Centre** — designed in traditional Najdi architecture, serves as an immersive exhibition venue for prospective investors.
- **Riyadh City-Scale Digital Twin** — 1,973 km² twin built in UE5 by Chameleon Interactive encompassing Diriyah, Qiddiya, and Green Riyadh. Displayed on 4m x 3m screen for stakeholder presentations.

### Opportunity for JV
Diriyah is the **ideal first JV target** because:
- Sapient already has the client relationship (DGDA)
- VMI has Saudi real estate digital twin experience
- The project is heritage + luxury + residential — perfect for immersive storytelling
- Off-plan residential sales need interactive 3D configurators
- Cultural tourism component needs engaging digital preview experiences
- The existing web platform (Sapient-built) can be enhanced with 3D/AI layers

### Proposed Solutions
1. **AI-Guided Heritage Explorer** — Conversational AI (Bodhi) guides visitors through a 3DGS capture of At-Turaif, telling the story in their language
2. **Residential Sales Twin** — Unreal Engine digital twin of residential districts with AI concierge for off-plan buyers
3. **Luna-Powered Sales Dashboard** — Real-time analytics on buyer engagement fed into Sapient's data platform

## 5.3 ROSHN

### The Project
ROSHN is PIF's national community developer, building integrated residential communities across Saudi Arabia. Projects include:
- **SEDRA** (Riyadh) — 20M+ sqm master-planned community
- **ALAROUS** (Jeddah) — waterfront community
- **ALFULWA** (Riyadh) — additional community
- **MARAFY** (Jeddah) — additional community
- **WAREFA** (Riyadh) — additional community

ROSHN focuses on quality-of-life communities with parks, schools, retail, and healthcare — targeting Saudi families.

### Digital Context (Highly Advanced)
ROSHN is the **most digitally advanced** PIF real estate entity:
- **50% of home sales now conducted online** via ROSHN.com — fully digital journey including identity verification via national digital IDs, electronic contract signing, online payments, real-time availability
- **AI-powered 3D models** of buildings for virtual planning already in production
- **Google Cloud partnership** to enhance AI capabilities for data-driven decision making
- **BIM (Building Information Modelling)** for virtual construction planning
- **ROSHNEXT** innovation programme — incubating PropTech ventures
- **ROSHN Hackathon 2025** — largest PropTech competition in the Middle East (SAR 1.3M prizes, 5 tracks: AI & Automation, Smart Infrastructure, IoT & Sustainability, Digital Lifestyle, Open Digital Solutions)
- **ROSHNTech app** combining home ownership, community services, smart living
- Partnerships with **what3words** (precise location addressing), **THIQAH** (digital notarisation), **SITE** (cybersecurity/cloud), **Jahez Group** (autonomous delivery)
- Multiple communities in simultaneous sales phases
- Domestic Saudi buyer audience (Arabic-first, mobile-first)
- Plans to develop **400,000+ homes** across **200M+ sqm land bank**, targeting SAR 312B GDP contribution by 2030

### Opportunity for JV
ROSHN's **scale and repeatability** make it ideal for a platform play:
- Multiple communities need the same core digital twin sales experience, localised per project
- High volume of units = high ROI on AI-automated sales qualification
- Smart community IoT data can feed into living digital twins post-handover

### Proposed Solutions
1. **ROSHN Sales Platform** — Templated digital twin sales experience deployable across all ROSHN communities, powered by UE5 Pixel Streaming
2. **AI Sales Qualifier** — Bodhi-powered agent that qualifies buyers, answers FAQ, schedules viewings, integrates with ROSHN's CRM
3. **Community Preview** — 3DGS captures of completed phases used to sell adjacent phases ("see what your neighbours already enjoy")
4. **Post-Handover Living Twin** — Digital twin of delivered community with IoT data for resident services (facility management, community events, maintenance requests)

## 5.4 Qiddiya

### The Project
Qiddiya is PIF's entertainment, sports, and arts giga-project — a 367km² destination southwest of Riyadh. Includes:
- **Six Flags Qiddiya** — the region's first Six Flags theme park (six themed lands with immersive tech)
- **Aquarabia** — water theme park
- **World's first Dragon Ball theme park**
- **Speed Park** — motorsport circuit
- **Performing Arts Centre** on Tuwaiq cliffs (800K visitors/year, ~260 performances/year, VR/AR/AI throughout)
- **Esports Arena** — 5,300 seats, largest combined video screen area of any esports arena, **4D haptic seats**, digital chandelier
- **Stadium with holographic technology** for live events
- **Golf courses, resort hotels, residential**
- Targeting 17M visits/year by 2030
- Skift/Qiddiya research: **86% of travellers now prioritise immersive experiences** over traditional sightseeing

### Digital Context
- **Globant PLAY LIFE partnership** — AI, data analytics, and cloud-powered digital ecosystem for visitor interaction (personalised booking, itinerary management, adventure discovery, community engagement)
- **IMERZA** has created large-scale Unreal Engine digital twins for Qiddiya (same team as Diriyah)
- Entertainment destination = **experience-first** marketing
- International tourist audience = multilingual, mobile-first
- AR, projection mapping, RFID, haptics already used in Qiddiya Gaming global campaigns
- Residential component needs off-plan sales tools

### Opportunity for JV
Qiddiya's **entertainment DNA** aligns perfectly with VMI/Journey's experiential background (TSX Broadway, immersive installations). Note: Globant is already a partner for the visitor platform — VMI+Sapient should position for the **physical experience and real estate components**, not compete with Globant on visitor app.

### Proposed Solutions
1. **Qiddiya Experience Preview** — Pixel-streamed Unreal Engine experience letting potential visitors "ride" attractions, explore hotels, and feel the destination before it opens
2. **AI Trip Planner** — Bodhi-powered conversational agent that helps visitors plan their Qiddiya trip, book tickets, select hotels, and personalise their itinerary
3. **Residential Sales Twin** — For Qiddiya's residential component, same intelligent sales experience as Diriyah/ROSHN
4. **In-Venue Digital Twin** — Post-opening, a live digital twin of the park for wayfinding, queue times, and personalised recommendations

## 5.5 NEOM

### Mirrana — NEOM's Cognitive Digital Twin PaaS
NEOM's technology subsidiary **Tonomus** has developed **Mirrana**, one of the most ambitious digital twin platforms in the world:
- **Cognitive digital twin PaaS** designed for the construction industry
- Integrates BIM, GIS, IoT, BMS, point clouds, photospheres, SCADA data
- Low-code, fully modular components for building custom digital twins
- Supports **Unreal Engine rendering + Pixel Streaming** for real-time browser visualisation
- Domain-agnostic composability — clients apply their own domain knowledge

### Mirrana Deployments
| NEOM Region | Application |
|-------------|------------|
| **Oxagon** | DTX (Digital Twin Experience) — immersive UE model + ArcGIS data, used for tenant attraction and economic discovery |
| **THE LINE** | Simulation twin for two modules — GIS + backend data for city-scale planning |
| **Design & Construction** | New industrialised construction platform |

### Scale of NEOM Infrastructure
- Tonomus Telecommunications Centre: servicing up to 60 locations, ~1,800 structures, ~300K construction staff by 2025, and **200 million IoT devices by 2030**
- **Tonomus + Oracle + NVIDIA** partnership for AI-powered innovation via planned Oracle Cloud Region at NEOM

### NEOM Venice Exhibition (Squint/Opera)
**"Flying Landscape"** — large-scale digital installation built in **Unreal Engine 5** enabling visitors to fly across the city. Showcased at Venice, communicating NEOM's "Zero Gravity Urbanism" concept.

### Existing Relationships
- **VMI/Journey**: Built the Trojena digital twin in Unreal Engine
- **Publicis Sapient**: Digital Enterprise Architecture consulting since Oct 2022

### Opportunity
Both companies are already engaged with NEOM independently. A JV could:
- Unify the architectural vision (Sapient) with the experiential delivery (VMI)
- Extend the Trojena digital twin with AI intelligence via Bodhi
- Apply the same platform across NEOM's other regions (THE LINE, Sindalah, Oxagon)
- **Key consideration**: NEOM already has Mirrana as an in-house digital twin platform — the JV should position as complementary (experiential/sales layer) rather than competing with Mirrana's construction/operations focus
