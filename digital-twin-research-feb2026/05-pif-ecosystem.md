# 5. PIF Real Estate Ecosystem — Diriyah, ROSHN, Qiddiya

## 5.1 Public Investment Fund (PIF) Overview

PIF is Saudi Arabia's sovereign wealth fund and the engine behind Vision 2030's diversification strategy. Its real estate portfolio includes some of the most ambitious development programmes in history, each requiring world-class digital sales, marketing, and visitor experience infrastructure.

### PIF Digital Transformation Context
- PIF giga-projects are entering **active sales and pre-leasing phases** in 2025-2026
- International buyer audiences require **remote, multilingual** sales tools
- Saudi Arabia is emerging as a **global leader in PropTech** adoption, alongside Dubai
- The Kingdom's emphasis on tourism (targeting 150M visits by 2030) demands **immersive digital previews** of destinations not yet built

---

## 5.2 Diriyah Gate Development Authority (DGDA)

### The Project
Diriyah is positioned as Saudi Arabia's cultural capital — a 14km² heritage-driven mixed-use destination at the birthplace of the Saudi state. It includes luxury hospitality, residential, retail, cultural institutions, and the UNESCO World Heritage site of At-Turaif.

### Digital Presence (Current)
- **Publicis Sapient** built 4 websites in 18 weeks (record-breaking Sitecore implementation)
- 1M+ visits in first 6 months
- 200+ components, 252 content types, 50+ templates

### Opportunity for JV
Diriyah is the **ideal first JV target** because:
- Sapient already has the client relationship (DGDA)
- VMI has Saudi real estate digital twin experience
- The project is heritage + luxury + residential — perfect for immersive storytelling
- Off-plan residential sales need interactive 3D configurators
- Cultural tourism component needs engaging digital preview experiences
- The existing web platform (Sapient-built) can be enhanced with 3D/AI layers

### Proposed Solutions
1. **AI-Guided Heritage Explorer** — Conversational AI (Bodhi) guides visitors through a 3DGS capture of At-Turaif, telling the story in their language
2. **Residential Sales Twin** — Unreal Engine digital twin of residential districts with AI concierge for off-plan buyers
3. **Luna-Powered Sales Dashboard** — Real-time analytics on buyer engagement fed into Sapient's data platform

## 5.3 ROSHN

### The Project
ROSHN is PIF's national community developer, building integrated residential communities across Saudi Arabia. Projects include:
- **SEDRA** (Riyadh) — 20M+ sqm master-planned community
- **ALAROUS** (Jeddah) — waterfront community
- **ALFULWA** (Riyadh) — additional community
- **MARAFY** (Jeddah) — additional community
- **WAREFA** (Riyadh) — additional community

ROSHN focuses on quality-of-life communities with parks, schools, retail, and healthcare — targeting Saudi families.

### Digital Context
- ROSHN has invested in **smart community technology** — IoT, digital infrastructure
- Multiple communities in simultaneous sales phases
- Domestic Saudi buyer audience (Arabic-first, mobile-first)
- Need for **scalable, repeatable** sales tools across communities

### Opportunity for JV
ROSHN's **scale and repeatability** make it ideal for a platform play:
- Multiple communities need the same core digital twin sales experience, localised per project
- High volume of units = high ROI on AI-automated sales qualification
- Smart community IoT data can feed into living digital twins post-handover

### Proposed Solutions
1. **ROSHN Sales Platform** — Templated digital twin sales experience deployable across all ROSHN communities, powered by UE5 Pixel Streaming
2. **AI Sales Qualifier** — Bodhi-powered agent that qualifies buyers, answers FAQ, schedules viewings, integrates with ROSHN's CRM
3. **Community Preview** — 3DGS captures of completed phases used to sell adjacent phases ("see what your neighbours already enjoy")
4. **Post-Handover Living Twin** — Digital twin of delivered community with IoT data for resident services (facility management, community events, maintenance requests)

## 5.4 Qiddiya

### The Project
Qiddiya is PIF's entertainment, sports, and arts giga-project — a 367km² destination southwest of Riyadh. Includes:
- **Six Flags Qiddiya** — the region's first Six Flags theme park
- **Speed Park** — motorsport circuit
- **Water Theme Park**
- **Golf courses, resort hotels, residential**
- Targeting 17M visits/year by 2030

### Digital Context
- Entertainment destination = **experience-first** marketing
- International tourist audience = multilingual, mobile-first
- Theme park and resort sales require **emotional, immersive preview**
- Residential component needs off-plan sales tools

### Opportunity for JV
Qiddiya's **entertainment DNA** aligns perfectly with VMI/Journey's experiential background (TSX Broadway, immersive installations):

### Proposed Solutions
1. **Qiddiya Experience Preview** — Pixel-streamed Unreal Engine experience letting potential visitors "ride" attractions, explore hotels, and feel the destination before it opens
2. **AI Trip Planner** — Bodhi-powered conversational agent that helps visitors plan their Qiddiya trip, book tickets, select hotels, and personalise their itinerary
3. **Residential Sales Twin** — For Qiddiya's residential component, same intelligent sales experience as Diriyah/ROSHN
4. **In-Venue Digital Twin** — Post-opening, a live digital twin of the park for wayfinding, queue times, and personalised recommendations
