# Genre Analysis: What Sells on Steam (2021-2026)

## Overview

Genre choice is one of the highest-leverage decisions a solo developer makes. The data from 2021-2025 shows that **9 out of 10 top genres remain stable year over year** — this isn't a fast-moving landscape. Core Steam audiences have consistent tastes.

The best indicator of genre viability isn't the single game that made the most money — it's how many games in that genre reach 1,000+ reviews. A genre with many moderately successful games has an "omnivorous" audience more likely to buy multiple titles.

---

## Genre Tier List (Solo Dev Viability)

### S-Tier: Proven, High-Volume Winners

#### Horror
- **#1 indie genre on Steam for 3+ consecutive years**
- Nearly doubled in size from 2024 to 2025
- 20 "hit" titles (1,000+ reviews) in H1 2024 alone
- Analyst recommendation: "Every indie should make a horror game"
- Solo-dev friendly: works with lo-fi graphics, short dev cycles, high streamability
- **Notable solo hits:** Lethal Company, Buckshot Roulette, Iron Lung, Content Warning

**Why horror works for solo devs:**
- Atmosphere > graphical fidelity (lo-fi and jank can enhance the horror)
- Short playtime is acceptable (2-4 hours is fine for horror)
- Inherently streamable — fear reactions drive views
- Low art asset requirements (dark environments, limited enemy types)
- Co-op horror is the hottest sub-genre (2023-2025)

#### Roguelike / Roguelike Deckbuilder
- Stable performer across the entire 5-year period
- 4 games with 1,000+ reviews in H1 2024 (deckbuilder sub-genre)
- Slay the Spire created a permanent audience for this
- **Notable solo hits:** Balatro, Vampire Survivors, Buckshot Roulette

**Why roguelikes work for solo devs:**
- Procedural generation extends content without hand-crafting levels
- Short run times create "one more run" addiction loops
- Mechanical depth from systems, not content volume
- Active modding communities extend lifespan

**Warning:** Vampire Survivors-style auto-shooters are now saturated. Only 1 hit in the sub-genre since early 2024.

---

### A-Tier: Strong Performers

#### Open World Survival Craft
- 16 games with 1,000+ reviews in H1 2024
- Core Steam audience staple
- "Crafty-buildy-games are still what the Steam audience wants"
- **Notable hits:** Valheim, Core Keeper, V Rising

**Solo dev challenge:** These games typically require substantial content and are harder to ship as a solo dev. Early Access is almost mandatory.

#### Simulation / Management / City Builder
- 7 Sim, 7 Management, 7 City Builder games with 1,000+ reviews in H1 2024
- Reliable, non-trendy — these genres just consistently perform
- **Notable solo hit:** Manor Lords

**Solo dev advantage:** These genres tolerate simpler graphics. Players care about systems, not visuals.

#### Co-op Multiplayer
- **Breakout trend of 2024-2025**
- Lethal Company (2023) triggered a wave of co-op games
- R.E.P.O., Content Warning, Chained Together, Peak all hit big in 2024-2025
- Social games have built-in viral marketing — every player recruits their friend group

**Solo dev challenge:** Networking code is hard. Testing requires multiple players. But the upside is enormous: every copy sold recruits 3-4 more buyers.

---

### B-Tier: Viable with the Right Execution

#### Narrative / Story-Driven
- **Fastest-growing genre on Steam**
- 2022: 17 narrative games hit 1,000+ reviews
- 2023: 20 narrative games hit 1,000+ reviews
- 2024: 30 narrative games hit 1,000+ reviews
- 2025: 8 narrative games hit 1,000+ reviews in early 2025 alone (doubling horror for that period)
- **Notable hits:** The Operator, Disco Elysium (small team), Papers Please

**Solo dev advantage:** Narrative games can be built with minimal graphics. Text + choices + atmosphere can carry the experience. Desktop sim / investigation games are a rising sub-niche.

#### Farming Sim / Life Sim
- Proven audience, steady performer
- Stardew Valley created a permanent market
- **Risk:** Players compare everything to Stardew Valley. You must differentiate.

#### Metroidvania
- Proven by Hollow Knight, but competitive
- Requires significant art and level design investment
- Long dev cycles (3+ years typical)

---

### C-Tier: Difficult / Avoid

#### 2D Platformers
- **Consistently underperform** on Steam
- Massively oversaturated
- "Greenshoots" exist in 3D platformers, but 2D is brutal

#### VR Games
- Underperform and show no signs of improvement
- Small install base limits revenue ceiling

#### Vampire Survivors Clones (Auto-Shooters)
- Genre is saturated as of 2024
- 17 hits in 2022-2023, but only 1 since early 2024 (Deep Rock Galactic Survivor — and that's an established IP)

## Emerging Genre Trends (2025-2026)

### Horror + [Anything] Hybrids
The most innovative hits combine horror with unexpected genres:
- **Horror + Gambling:** Buckshot Roulette
- **Horror + Co-op Work Sim:** Lethal Company
- **Horror + Social Deduction:** Content Warning
- These hybrids tap horror's streamability while adding a novel twist

### "Cozy" Games with Depth
- Games that look relaxing but have deep systems
- Spilled! (clean up oil spills with a boat), Rusty's Retirement (idle farming)
- Often succeed at ultra-low price points ($5-8)

### Desktop Sims / Software-as-Game
- The Operator, Her Story, Obra Dinn established this niche
- Players interact with fictional software interfaces
- Extremely solo-dev friendly — UI is the art

### Chance / Luck-Based Roguelikes
- Balatro's success spawned many "random chance meets roguelike" games
- Pachinko, slots, stock trading — all being dressed in roguelike mechanics
- "These games are very streamable, and they have plenty of timesink factor"

## Genre Selection Framework for Solo Devs

Ask yourself:
1. **Can I make this alone?** (Horror: yes. MMO: no.)
2. **Is the audience "omnivorous"?** (Do they buy many games in this genre, or just one?)
3. **Does it work with simple/lo-fi graphics?** (Management sim: yes. Open world RPG: hard.)
4. **Is it inherently streamable?** (Will funny/dramatic moments happen naturally?)
5. **Can I ship a complete version in 1-2 years?** (Roguelikes: yes. Metroidvania: maybe not.)
6. **Is there an underserved niche within the genre?** (Poker roguelike didn't exist before Balatro.)

The ideal solo-dev game sits at the intersection of: a genre Steam's audience loves + an art style you can execute alone + a mechanic that creates shareable moments + a scope you can actually finish.
