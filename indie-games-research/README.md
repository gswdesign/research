## Key Stat
- Indie games made up **48% of all Steam revenue** in 2024
- The median indie game earns **~$4,000 lifetime** on Steam
- Only **0.5%** of indie games released in 2024 are financially viable
- The top 1% of indies earn **90%** of total indie revenue

The gap between hits and the rest is enormous. This research examines what separates them.
