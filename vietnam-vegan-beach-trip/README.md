# Vietnam Vegan Beach Trip - April/May 2026

## Trip Overview
- **Who:** Sisters trip
- **When:** April/May 2026
- **Vibe:** Laid-back vegan beach holiday with 1-2 days of city exploration
- **Focus:** 5-star vegan-friendly resorts, beach locations, explorable areas

## Quick Recommendation

**Base yourself in the Da Nang / Hoi An corridor (Central Vietnam)** for 7-10 days, with an optional 2-day city buzz trip to Ho Chi Minh City.

### Why This Works
1. **Best weather in Vietnam** during April/May (dry, sunny, 28-32C)
2. **Most vegan-friendly beach area** in the country
3. **World-class 5-star resorts** with dedicated vegan menus
4. **Incredible day trips** - ancient towns, islands, marble mountains
5. **Da Nang** gives you the hustle & bustle fix without needing to fly elsewhere
6. **Hoi An** is the laid-back beach/culture gem
