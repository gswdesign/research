## BOTTOM LINE

**Four Seasons and Six Senses blow the £2k budget** - they're £460-700/night before you even add flights. Beautiful but not happening at this budget.

**Your best bet for £2k or under:**
1. **Fusion Resort Da Nang** (best value 5-star, spa included) - easily under £2k
2. **Marriott Da Nang** (solid 5-star, beautiful beach) - easily under £2k
3. **Mix of both** for variety - still under £2k
4. **Add InterContinental for 2-3 nights** if you want to splurge on the vegan Michelin experience - pushes toward £2k

**Cheapest time in May:** Mid-to-late May, mid-week check-in (Tue-Thu)
