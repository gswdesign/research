# Best Vegan-Friendly Beach Destinations - Ranked

## #1: HOI AN (An Bang Beach) - TOP PICK

**Why it's perfect for you:**
- Laid-back, charming ancient town (UNESCO World Heritage) right near the beach
- An Bang Beach has beach clubs, restaurants, sunset vibes
- One of Vietnam's most vegan-friendly towns - strong Buddhist food tradition
- Incredible for exploring: ancient town, lantern-lit streets, custom tailoring, cooking classes
- Cosy and relaxed, not party-focused
- 30-40 min from Da Nang for your "hustle and bustle" days

**Vegan scene:** Excellent. Numerous fully vegan restaurants, vegan beach brunches, plant-based cooking classes. Hoi An has a strong tradition of vegetarian/vegan Buddhist cuisine.

**Beach vibe:** An Bang Beach - relaxed, beach clubs with cushions and cocktails, sunset bars. Not overcrowded.

---

## #2: DA NANG (My Khe Beach)

**Why it works:**
- Vietnam's third-largest city - this is your "hustle and bustle" destination
- My Khe Beach is one of the most beautiful urban beaches in the world
- Excellent vegan restaurant scene, especially around the My An area
- Great smoothie bowls, healthy plant-based cafes
- Perfect base for exploring Marble Mountains, Ba Na Hills, Hai Van Pass
- Only 30-40 min from Hoi An - easy to combine both

**Vegan scene:** Very good. Lots of fully vegan restaurants concentrated in My An area. Known for excellent smoothie bowls and healthy eating.

**Beach vibe:** Wide, long beach. More urban/energetic than An Bang but still lovely.

---

## #3: NHA TRANG

**Why it's great:**
- 300+ days of sunshine per year
- Feb-May is peak ideal season
- Beautiful long beach, scuba diving, island hopping
- More of a party/resort town vibe
- Vegan versions of local seafood specialties (soy-based fish alternatives, seaweed)

**Vegan scene:** Decent. Not as many dedicated vegan spots as Da Nang/Hoi An but manageable. Hotels cater well.

**Beach vibe:** Long stretch of beach, resort-lined, livelier nightlife scene.

---

## #4: PHU QUOC (Island)

**Why consider it:**
- Tropical island paradise, stunning beaches
- Several 5-star resorts with vegan options (Fusion Resort, Regent)
- Great for a "do nothing" beach segment
- Phu Quoc Eco Beach Resort specifically praised by vegan travellers

**Caution for your dates:** May starts the rainy season here. April is fine but May gets afternoon showers. Best to visit in April if including Phu Quoc.

---

## #5: HO CHI MINH CITY (Your City Buzz Stop)

**Not a beach destination** but the #1 most vegan-friendly city in Vietnam (and all of Asia).
- 130+ fully vegan restaurants
- Incredible street food scene with vegan options everywhere
- Bustling markets, historic sites, rooftop bars
- Perfect for your 1-2 days of city energy

**Vegan scene:** Unmatched. PETA rates it top 10 in Asia. More vegan restaurants than most European capitals.

---

## Recommended Combo

> **Hoi An/An Bang Beach (5-6 nights)** + **Da Nang (2-3 nights)** + **HCMC (1-2 nights)**

This gives you the perfect mix: laid-back beach vibes, explorable charm, city buzz, and wall-to-wall vegan food.
