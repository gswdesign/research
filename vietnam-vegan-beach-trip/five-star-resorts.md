# 5-Star Vegan-Friendly Resorts

## TOP PICK: Four Seasons Resort The Nam Hai, Hoi An

**Location:** Between Da Nang and Hoi An, beachfront
**Stars:** 5-star (consistently rated #1 in Vietnam)
**Why it's the one:**
- Dedicated **"Glow With Food" fully plant-based menu** at Cafe Nam Hai
- Farm-to-table vegan dining using ingredients from their own on-site organic farm (40+ varieties of herbs/plants)
- Vegan menu always available at both La Sen and Cafe Nam Hai restaurants
- Menu highlights: Super Green Pasta, Indian Thali, Vegan Vietnamese Pancake
- **Unique 2026 experience:** Artisan-led tofu-making workshop in rice fields, including a full vegan meal with a host from a tofu-making village
- Restaurants: NAYUU (Japanese omakase), Cafe Nam Hai (international/Indian), La Sen (Vietnamese-French)
- Stunning beachfront, world-class spa, between Hoi An old town and Da Nang

**Vegan confidence: VERY HIGH** - they have a dedicated vegan dining programme, not just "we can accommodate"

[Four Seasons Nam Hai Vegan Menu](https://www.fourseasons.com/hoian/dining/restaurants/cafe-nam-hai/vegan/)

---

## RUNNER-UP: InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula Resort

**Location:** Son Tra Peninsula, Da Nang (jungle mountainside meets private beach)
**Stars:** 5-star
**Why it's incredible:**
- Described as "the very best in vegan luxury, not only in Vietnam, but in the whole of South East Asia"
- **La Maison 1888** - Michelin-starred French fine dining with a dedicated multi-course vegan tasting menu (3+ hour gourmet vegan feast)
- Dishes like "Pineapple with Mushroom", "Parsnip with Truffle", "Avocado and Eggplant"
- **Barefoot** - beachside dining with vegan Mediterranean/Arabic options
- **Citron** - Vietnamese restaurant with vegan options
- **Tingara** - Japanese cuisine by Michelin Chef Junichi Yoshida
- Club InterContinental Lounge serves vegan canapes with cocktails
- Vegan spa treatments available
- Reached by private funicular through monkey-filled jungle
- Designed by legendary architect Bill Bensley

**Vegan confidence: VERY HIGH** - multiple reviewers call it the best vegan luxury experience in SE Asia

[Vegan Food Quest Review](https://www.veganfoodquest.com/hotel-resort-reviews/vietnam/intercontinental-danang-sun-peninsula-resort/)

---

## GREAT OPTION: Fusion Resort & Villas Da Nang

**Location:** Between Da Nang and Hoi An, beachfront
**Stars:** 5-star
**Why it works:**
- Famous "all-spa inclusive" concept - daily spa treatments included in room rate
- Award-winning MAIA Spa (2,800 sqm) - Global Winner for Luxury Beach Resort Spa 2025
- Two restaurants with vegan menus: 'Five' (main) and 'Fresh' (poolside/beachside)
- Creative vegan breakfasts: DIY cereal bar with organic brown rice, flax seeds, fresh fruit, soya milk
- Vegan-friendly spa products (Natural Rendez Vous, plant-based, cruelty-free)
- Non-feather pillows, vegan in-room amenities
- Infrared saunas, herbal steam baths, yoga studio
- Wellness-focused vibe perfect for a relaxing sisters trip

**Vegan confidence: HIGH** - they go beyond food into fully vegan-conscious hospitality

[Vegan Food Quest Review](https://www.veganfoodquest.com/hotel-resort-reviews/vietnam/fusion-maia-danang/)

## ALSO WORTH CONSIDERING

### Alma Resort Cam Ranh (near Nha Trang)
- 5-star, 14 dining venues, spacious villas
- On the vegan-friendly resort list
- Great for families, has a Splash Water Park
- 30-60% cheaper than Bali/Maldives for comparable luxury

### Regent Phu Quoc
- Trades #1 spot with Four Seasons in 2026 guest-voted awards
- Stunning island resort
- Best visited in April (May = rainy season starts)

### Evason Ana Mandara (Nha Trang)
- Listed as vegan-friendly by Vegan Food Quest
- Beachfront in central Nha Trang

## Price Tips
- Vietnam 5-star resorts are **30-60% cheaper than Maldives or Bali** for comparable luxury
- Many resorts offer **4 nights for the price of 3** promotions in 2026
- **May** is shoulder season = 30-50% lower rates than April
- Free half-board deals available at several properties

---

## My Recommendation

> **Split your stay:**
> - **Four Seasons Nam Hai** for the ultimate vegan beach luxury (Hoi An side)
> - **InterContinental Danang** for 2-3 nights of jungle-meets-beach glamour + Michelin vegan dining
> - Both are in the Da Nang/Hoi An area, so you get variety without long transfers
