# Flights & Resort Prices - London to Vietnam, May 2026

## FLIGHTS: London to Da Nang

No direct flights exist - all routes require 1 stop (Dubai, Singapore, Bangkok, KL, etc).

### Cheapest Options Found

| Route | Price (return) | Airline | Notes |
|-------|---------------|---------|-------|
| London to Da Nang | **from ~£379-£500** | Malaysia Airlines, Emirates | Via KL or Dubai |
| London to HCMC (then domestic to DAD) | **from ~£349-£400** | China Eastern, various | Cheapest Vietnam route from London |
| London to Da Nang (Google Flights example) | **£706** | Emirates | 23 Apr - 6 May |
| London to Da Nang (Google Flights example) | **£1,003** | EVA Air | 2 May - 15 May |

### How to Get the Best Price

1. **Fly mid-week** (Tuesday/Wednesday departures) - saves £50-100+
2. **Fly afternoon** - cheapest time of day
3. **Book ~60 days ahead** (so book by early March for May travel)
4. **Consider flying into HCMC first** (cheapest route) then domestic flight to Da Nang (~£30-50 one way on VietJet)
5. **May is shoulder season** - avg ~£548 return, but deals from £379
6. **Check these sites for best deals:**
   - [Skyscanner](https://www.skyscanner.net/routes/lond/dad/london-to-da-nang.html)
   - [KAYAK](https://www.kayak.co.uk/flight-routes/London-LON/Da-Nang-DAD)
   - [Google Flights](https://www.google.com/travel/flights/flights-from-london-to-da-nang.html)
   - [Momondo](https://www.momondo.co.uk/flights/london/vietnam)

### Flight Time
- Shortest: ~15 hours (1 stop)
- Typical: 16-20 hours depending on layover

### Internal Flights (if doing HCMC + Da Nang)
- HCMC to Da Nang: ~1.5hrs, £30-50 on VietJet or Vietnam Airlines
- Very frequent departures throughout the day

### Key Notes on Each

#### Fusion Resort & Villas Da Nang - BEST VALUE
- **Incredible value** - 5-star with daily spa INCLUDED from ~$104/night
- 187 rooms, beachfront between Da Nang and Hoi An
- Free breakfast, WiFi, parking
- Award-winning MAIA Spa (2,800 sqm)
- Vegan-friendly food and spa products
- Damage deposit of VND 2,000,000 (~$80) on arrival, refunded at checkout

#### Four Seasons Nam Hai - BEST VEGAN EXPERIENCE
- Starts from ~$581/night in May
- All rooms are villas - one king bed, **twin beds available on request**
- Dedicated "Glow With Food" vegan menu, on-site organic farm
- Trundle bed available for extra sleeping
- Book direct on [fourseasons.com](https://www.fourseasons.com/hoian/) for best rates

#### InterContinental Danang - BEST ALL-ROUNDER
- From ~$380/night (KAYAK recent low) - great for this level of luxury
- 189 rooms, Michelin-starred restaurant with vegan tasting menu
- Twin beds likely available on request
- IHG Rewards members get better rates
- Book on [IHG.com](https://www.ihg.com/intercontinental/hotels/us/en/danang-city/dadha/hoteldetail)

#### Six Senses Ninh Van Bay - MOST EXCLUSIVE
- Only accessible by boat - total seclusion
- 62 private pool villas (1-3 bedrooms available)
- **May is their cheapest month!** Expect ~$676-889/night
- Current deals: 15% off rates for travel through May 2026, or free 4th night
- Near Nha Trang (not Da Nang area - would need separate travel)
- Book on [sixsenses.com](https://www.sixsenses.com/en/hotels-resorts/asia-the-pacific/vietnam/ninh-van-bay/)

#### Six Senses Con Dao - REMOTE PARADISE
- On its own island, 1.6km private beach
- Stay 4+ nights = up to 35% off
- Stay 3+ nights = **complimentary flights from HCMC**
- 50 private pool villas
- National Geographic "Best Eco Lodge in the World"

#### Danang Marriott Resort & Spa - SOLID LUXURY, GOOD PRICE
- Non Nuoc Beach location (Forbes "most beautiful beach")
- Near Marble Mountains
- 15 min from Da Nang airport
- **Non Nuoc Beach Villas** property has 2-4 bedroom villas with dedicated twin rooms
- Marriott Bonvoy members: 5% discount + free one-way airport transfer with villa booking
