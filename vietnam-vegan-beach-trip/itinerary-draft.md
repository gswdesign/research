# Draft Itinerary - 10-12 Days

## Option A: The Perfect 10 Days (Beach Focus + City Buzz)

### Days 1-2: Ho Chi Minh City (The Buzz)
- Arrive HCMC, settle in
- Explore District 1: Ben Thanh Market, Notre-Dame Cathedral, Central Post Office
- Hit the vegan restaurant scene (130+ options!)
- Rooftop drinks at sunset
- Optional: Cu Chi Tunnels half-day trip
- **Stay:** Hotel des Arts Saigon or Park Hyatt Saigon

### Day 3: Fly to Da Nang -> Transfer to Hoi An
- Morning flight HCMC to Da Nang (~1.5hrs)
- Transfer to Hoi An (30-40 min)
- Afternoon: settle in, An Bang Beach sunset
- **Check in:** Four Seasons The Nam Hai

### Days 4-6: Hoi An Beach & Explore (Laid Back)
- **Day 4:** An Bang Beach day - beach club, swimming, vegan lunch on the sand, sunset cocktails
- **Day 5:** Hoi An Ancient Town - morning walk, custom tailoring fitting, vegan cooking class, lantern-lit evening stroll
- **Day 6:** Cham Islands day trip (snorkeling, island beach) OR Marble Mountains morning + beach afternoon
- Vegan restaurants in town for dinners
- "Glow With Food" vegan dining at Four Seasons

### Day 7: Transfer to InterContinental Danang
- Morning: pick up your tailored clothes in Hoi An!
- Transfer to InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula
- Afternoon: private beach, jungle funicular, explore the resort
- Evening: Multi-course vegan tasting menu at La Maison 1888 (Michelin star)
- **Check in:** InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula

### Days 8-9: Da Nang (Beach + City Energy)
- **Day 8:** Ba Na Hills & Golden Bridge day trip, evening at Dragon Bridge (fire-breathing on weekends!)
- **Day 9:** Beach morning at My Khe, Marble Mountains if not done, Da Nang street food tour, Han Market, night market
- Vegan restaurant hopping in My An area

### Day 10: Departure
- Morning beach/spa time
- Transfer to Da Nang airport
- Fly home or onward

---

## Option B: Extended 12 Days (Add Nha Trang or Phu Quoc)

Same as above but add:

### Days 10-12: Nha Trang OR Phu Quoc
- **Nha Trang** (short flight from Da Nang): 300 days of sunshine, island hopping, diving, beach relaxation
- **Phu Quoc** (flight via HCMC): tropical island, April only ideally, Fusion Resort Phu Quoc or Regent Phu Quoc
- Pure beach chill to end the trip

---

## Option C: Flip It - Beach First, City Last

If you'd rather end with the buzz:
1. Fly into Da Nang (Days 1-7: Hoi An + Da Nang beach)
2. Fly to HCMC (Days 8-9: city exploration)
3. Fly home from HCMC

This works well if your return flight connects through a southern hub.

## Budget Rough Guide (per person)

| Category | Budget Range |
|----------|-------------|
| Flights (international) | Depends on origin |
| Internal flights (HCMC-Da Nang) | $50-100 USD |
| Four Seasons Nam Hai (per night) | $400-800 USD |
| InterContinental Danang (per night) | $300-600 USD |
| Fusion Resort (per night, spa included) | $250-500 USD |
| Meals outside resorts | $5-20 USD per meal |
| Day trips/activities | $20-80 USD per activity |
| Custom tailoring in Hoi An | $50-200 USD per garment |

**Vietnam is 30-60% cheaper than Bali/Maldives for equivalent 5-star luxury.**
**May bookings are 30-50% cheaper than April.**

## Booking Notes
- Book resorts ASAP - April/May is popular season for Central Vietnam
- Many resorts have "4 nights for 3" promotions in 2026
- Internal flights are cheap - book on VietJet or Vietnam Airlines
- Visa: check e-visa requirements for your nationality (most can get e-visa online)
