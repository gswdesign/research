# Things To Do - Day Trips & Exploring

## BEACH & CHILL

### An Bang Beach (Hoi An)
- Laid-back beach clubs with cushions, cocktails, and music
- Beach restaurants serving food right on the sand
- Sunset drinks - the main vibe
- Less crowded than My Khe, more bohemian

### My Khe Beach (Da Nang)
- Wide, long stretch of sand - great for morning walks
- Water sports: surfing, paddleboarding, jet skis
- Beachfront cafes and bars

## EXPLORE & ADVENTURE (Day Trips from Hoi An / Da Nang)

### Hoi An Ancient Town (UNESCO World Heritage)
- Wander lantern-lit streets of this 15th-19th century trading port
- Visit temples, Japanese covered bridge, assembly halls
- **Custom tailoring** - get clothes made in 24-48hrs (amazing sisters activity!)
- Central Market for street food
- Best at night when lanterns light up
- Rent bikes and cycle around

### Marble Mountains (40 min from Hoi An)
- Five marble and limestone hills named after the elements
- Caves, pagodas, panoramic views
- Takes 2-3 hours to explore properly
- Entrance: ~40,000 VND (very cheap)

### Cham Islands (Boat trip from Hoi An)
- UNESCO biosphere reserve
- Snorkeling, diving, island beaches
- Can do as a day trip or overnight
- Best snorkeling in the region

### Ba Na Hills & Golden Bridge
- The famous bridge held up by giant stone hands
- 1,414m elevation - cool mountain air
- World-record cable car ride
- French colonial village, gardens, theme park
- Full day trip

### My Son Sanctuary (1 hour from Hoi An)
- 1,300-year-old Hindu temple ruins hidden in the jungle
- Built by the Cham people
- Vietnam's answer to Angkor Wat (smaller but less crowded)
- Best visited early morning

### Hai Van Pass
- "The most beautiful coastal road in the world" (Jeremy Clarkson)
- 21km stretch connecting Da Nang to Hue
- Can do by motorbike with a driver, or private car
- Stop at fishing villages and viewpoints along the way

### Tam Thanh Mural Village
- Hidden gem south of Hoi An
- Entire village covered in colourful murals
- Great for photos and wandering
- Not touristy

## COOKING & CULTURE (Perfect for Sisters)

### Vegan Cooking Classes
- Several options in Hoi An for plant-based Vietnamese cooking
- Typically includes market tour + cooking + eating
- Learn to make vegan pho, banh mi, spring rolls, banh xeo

### Lantern Making Workshop
- Hoi An is famous for its silk lanterns
- Make your own to take home

### Custom Tailoring
- Hoi An is THE place in Vietnam for bespoke clothing
- Get dresses, suits, shoes made to measure
- Usually 1-2 fittings over 24-48 hours
- Very affordable compared to Western prices

### Cycling Tours
- Ride through rice paddies, fishing villages, and countryside
- Several guided tours available from Hoi An

## YOUR "HUSTLE & BUSTLE" DAYS

### Option A: Da Nang City (30-40 min from Hoi An)
- Dragon Bridge (breathes fire on weekend nights!)
- Han Market - bustling local market
- Street food scene
- Rooftop bars
- Night markets
- Feels like a real Vietnamese city, not a tourist town

### Option B: Ho Chi Minh City (1.5hr flight from Da Nang)
- Ben Thanh Market
- War Remnants Museum
- Cu Chi Tunnels (half day trip)
- District 1 walking - French colonial architecture
- Rooftop bars (Saigon Saigon, Chill Skybar)
- **130+ vegan restaurants** - the most vegan-friendly city in Asia
- Buzzy motorbike-filled streets, the real deal
