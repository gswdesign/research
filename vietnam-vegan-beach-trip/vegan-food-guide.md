# Vegan Food Guide - Vietnam

## Why Vietnam is Amazing for Vegans

Vietnam has a deep Buddhist tradition where many people eat fully vegan on certain days of the religious calendar (1st and 15th of the lunar month), and some eat vegan year-round. This means veganized versions of almost every local dish exist and are widely available. Look for restaurants with **"chay"** in the name - this means vegetarian/vegan.

---

## Key Vietnamese Phrases

| English | Vietnamese |
|---------|-----------|
| I am vegan | Toi an chay |
| Vegan/vegetarian food | Com chay / An chay |
| No meat | Khong thit |
| No fish sauce | Khong nuoc mam |
| No eggs | Khong trung |
| No milk | Khong sua |
| Vegan restaurant | Nha hang chay |
| Thank you | Cam on |

**Important:** "Chay" restaurants are everywhere and serve fully plant-based food. Even small towns have them.

## Must-Try Vegan Vietnamese Dishes

### Soups & Noodles
- **Pho chay** - vegan pho with vegetable/mushroom broth, tofu, herbs
- **Bun bo Hue chay** - spicy lemongrass noodle soup (vegan version)
- **Mi Quang chay** - Hoi An's signature turmeric noodle dish (vegan)

### Street Food
- **Banh mi chay** - vegan baguette with tofu, pate, pickled veg, herbs
- **Banh xeo chay** - crispy turmeric crepe with bean sprouts & mushrooms
- **Goi cuon chay** - fresh spring rolls with tofu and vegetables
- **Banh cuon chay** - steamed rice rolls with mushrooms

### Rice Dishes
- **Com chay** - vegan rice plate with multiple side dishes (the classic Buddhist lunch)
- **Com tam chay** - broken rice with vegan protein

### Sweets
- **Che** - sweet dessert soups (many are naturally vegan - coconut milk, beans, tapioca)
- **Banh chuoi** - banana cake
- Fresh tropical fruit everywhere: dragon fruit, mango, rambutan, mangosteen

### Drinks
- **Ca phe sua dau nanh** - Vietnamese coffee with soy milk
- **Sinh to** - fresh fruit smoothies (ask without dairy)
- Fresh coconut water - available everywhere

## Restaurant Tips by Location

### Hoi An
- Many restaurants along the river and in the ancient town offer vegan menus
- An Bang Beach has vegan-friendly beach restaurants
- Look for cooking classes specifically offering vegan Vietnamese cuisine
- Buddhist restaurants near temples serve excellent cheap vegan food

### Da Nang
- **My An area** is the vegan hub - most vegan restaurants clustered here
- Known for excellent smoothie bowls and healthy bowls
- Check HappyCow app for latest listings

### Ho Chi Minh City
- 130+ fully vegan restaurants - use HappyCow app
- District 1 and District 3 have the highest concentration
- Street food stalls with "chay" signs are everywhere
- Vegan banh mi stalls are common

---

## Tips for Eating Vegan in Vietnam

1. **Download HappyCow** - essential app for finding vegan restaurants
2. **"Chay" restaurants** are your safest bet for fully plant-based food
3. **Fish sauce (nuoc mam)** is the main thing to watch out for - it's in many dishes. Always specify "khong nuoc mam"
4. **Buddhist "chay" buffets** are incredibly cheap (often $1-2 USD) and delicious
5. **Hotel restaurants** at 5-star properties will always accommodate with advance notice
6. **Markets** have fruit stalls with incredible tropical fruit at tiny prices
7. **Coffee shops** increasingly offer oat milk and soy milk, especially in tourist areas
8. **Mock meats** are common in Vietnamese vegan cooking - don't be surprised by very realistic "chicken" or "pork" made from soy/wheat gluten
