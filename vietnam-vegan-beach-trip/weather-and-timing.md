# Weather & Timing - April/May 2026

## TL;DR
**April** = best all-round month, perfect everywhere. **May** = still excellent for Central Vietnam beaches (Da Nang, Hoi An, Nha Trang), slightly cheaper, fewer crowds. South starts getting afternoon showers in May.

---

## By Region

### Central Vietnam (DA NANG / HOI AN / NHA TRANG) - BEST BET
| | April | May |
|---|---|---|
| Weather | Dry, sunny, warm | Hot, mostly dry |
| Temp | 26-30C | 28-32C |
| Rain | Very low | Low |
| Crowds | Moderate-high | Lower (shoulder season) |
| Prices | Higher | 30-50% cheaper |

- Central Vietnam's dry season runs March-August - you're right in the sweet spot
- Nha Trang gets 300+ days of sunshine per year (most in Vietnam)
- Feb-May is considered the ideal window for Nha Trang

### Southern Vietnam (PHU QUOC / HO CHI MINH CITY)
| | April | May |
|---|---|---|
| Weather | End of dry season | Rainy season begins |
| Temp | 30-33C | 30-33C |
| Rain | Low | Afternoon showers (short) |

- Phu Quoc: April is fine, May gets wetter but showers are short
- HCMC: Hot and humid but manageable for 1-2 city days

### Northern Vietnam (HANOI / HA LONG BAY)
| | April | May |
|---|---|---|
| Weather | Warm, pleasant | Hot, humid, increasing rain |
| Temp | 24-28C | 27-32C |

- Not recommended as beach base but fine for a city stop

---

## Recommendation

**Go in late April** for the best weather across all regions, or **early-mid May** for better deals and fewer tourists while Central Vietnam beaches are still perfect.
